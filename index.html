Team DNA · WIUT Hackathon 2026 · Computer vision track

Traffic event detection from a fixed camera

Our pipeline finds 14 kinds of traffic events in CCTV video and scores the risk of a collision in every frame. The road layout is inferred from each video, so nothing is drawn by hand.

Open the explorer Try the demo
01Approach

Approach

Part A returns every event in a video as [start, end, label]. Part B reads frames one at a time and returns a collision risk for each, using only the frames seen so far.

pre-trained model computed from each video rules

How each part works

PartHowLearned or rule
Road usersYOLO11-L finds cars, buses, trucks, motorcycles, bicycles and people on every 2nd frame; ByteTrack links them into tracks.pre-trained model
Road layoutA background image without traffic is built from the video. Mask2Former marks road, kerbs and paint on it, and where vehicles and people move gives the carriageway, crossings, stop lines, solid lane lines and the junction.computed from each video
Traffic lightThe lamps of each signal head are read in every frame. The head whose changes match when traffic starts from the stop line is the one that controls it.computed from each video
ScaleThe width of cars at each point of the image gives a ruler, so speeds and distances are in car widths and need no camera calibration.computed from each video
11 event classesOne rule per class, written from the task's definitions, applied to the tracks and the layout.rules
Accident, fire, obstacleCandidates come from tracks touching, a fire detector, objects that appear on the road, and a check of the whole frame every few seconds. Qwen3-VL-2B must answer yes on frames of the video before one is reported.pre-trained models and rules
Collision riskA separate detector and tracker. The score rises when two road users are on a collision course.rules

Where road users go

EDA

VideoLightResolutionfpsDurationCars / framePeople / frame
  1. Queues follow the traffic light.

    On red, standing vehicles build up behind the near stop line; on green they clear. This is how the pipeline finds which signal head controls the approach.

  2. Cars wait inside the junction.

    Cars stand in the middle of the junction for up to 45 s, waiting for their turn to move on. This is normal here, so it is not reported as a stopped vehicle.

  3. The camera is not in the same place in every video.

    C3902 is shifted and zoomed compared with C3896, so a layout drawn once by hand would be off. The layout is inferred for each video instead.

  4. Cars near the camera look almost four times wider than far away.

    Speeds and distances are measured in car widths, so the same thresholds work across the whole image.
